## The Brand

Henzadem is a Kuwait-based specialty retailer with a loyal customer base built through word-of-mouth. Moving their business online meant finding a way to preserve the personal touch while scaling reach.

## What We Built

We developed an e-commerce solution that balances simplicity for customers with operational depth for the team:

- **Clean, focused storefront** — uncluttered browsing experience that highlights the product range
- **Order management** — full order lifecycle from placement through fulfilment and tracking
- **Customer accounts** — saved orders, easy reorders, and address book
- **Arabic-first design** — RTL layout built as the default, not an afterthought
- **Inventory control** — real-time stock levels with low-stock alerts for the operations team

## The Outcome

Henzadem's online channel opened a new revenue stream without disrupting existing customer relationships. The store now handles a growing share of their total sales volume.
