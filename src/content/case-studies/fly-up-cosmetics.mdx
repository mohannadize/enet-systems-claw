## The Brand

Fly Up Cosmetics is a Kuwait-based beauty brand targeting a younger, trend-conscious demographic. They needed an online store that matched their energetic brand identity and delivered a smooth shopping experience on mobile.

## What We Built

We designed and developed a full e-commerce platform tailored to the cosmetics category:

- **Visually immersive product pages** — high-impact imagery with shade selection and ingredient highlights
- **Shade finder functionality** — guided product discovery for foundation and complexion products
- **Fast mobile checkout** — minimal-step flow optimised for impulse purchases
- **Social proof integration** — customer reviews and UGC gallery on product pages
- **Abandoned cart recovery** — automated follow-up sequences to recover lost sales
- **Promotions engine** — limited-time offers, gift-with-purchase, and bundle discounts

## The Outcome

Fly Up Cosmetics launched with a store that immediately reflected their brand character. Mobile conversion rates outperformed regional cosmetics benchmarks from the first month of operation.
