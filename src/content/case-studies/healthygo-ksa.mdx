## The Brand

HealthyGo is a Saudi Arabia-based distributor of health, wellness, and nutritional products. Rapid growth had stretched their manual systems to breaking point — tracking inventory across multiple SKUs and managing supplier relationships in spreadsheets was no longer sustainable.

## What We Implemented

We deployed a comprehensive Odoo ERP solution tailored to the distribution and health sector:

- **Inventory management** — lot/serial number tracking, expiry date management, and FEFO picking rules critical for health products
- **Procurement & supplier management** — purchase order automation, vendor pricelist management, and goods receipt workflows
- **Sales & distribution** — order processing, delivery route planning, and proof of delivery
- **Accounting** — full financial suite with ZATCA-compliant VAT reporting for KSA regulations
- **Reporting & dashboards** — real-time visibility into stock levels, sales performance, and cash flow

## The Outcome

HealthyGo's operations team moved from reactive firefighting to proactive management. Stock-out incidents dropped significantly, and the finance team closed monthly books days faster than before the implementation.
