## The Brand

Wafi is a well-established Kuwait general trading company with diverse product lines spanning consumer goods, FMCG, and specialty retail. With physical presence already solid, they needed a digital infrastructure worthy of their scale.

## What We Delivered

### E-commerce Platform

We built Wafi's consumer-facing online store from scratch — a high-performance platform designed to handle their broad catalogue with ease:

- **Multi-category catalogue** — structured product taxonomy across hundreds of SKUs
- **Inventory integration** — live stock counts pulled directly from warehouse data
- **Promotional engine** — flash sales, bundle deals, and seasonal campaigns
- **Kuwait delivery integration** — local logistics API integration for real-time delivery slots
- **Mobile-optimised** — 70%+ of their traffic comes from mobile devices

### Corporate Website

The Wafi corporate site serves a different audience: B2B buyers, distributors, and institutional clients. We built a professional presence that communicates credibility and scale:

- Company overview and division structure
- Product portfolio with wholesale inquiry flows
- Media & press section
- Partnership and distributor contact channels

### Odoo ERP Implementation

Wafi's operational complexity required an enterprise-grade ERP. We implemented a comprehensive Odoo deployment covering:

- **Multi-warehouse inventory** — stock tracking across multiple physical locations
- **Procurement workflows** — supplier management, purchase orders, and goods receipt
- **Sales & distribution** — order management from inquiry to fulfilment
- **Financial management** — full accounting, VAT compliance, and reporting dashboards
- **E-commerce sync** — seamless bridge between the online store and the operational backend

## The Outcome

Wafi now operates with a fully integrated digital stack. Orders placed online flow directly into Odoo, inventory updates propagate to the store in real time, and the corporate site brings in qualified B2B leads that the sales team can act on immediately. Manual reconciliation between systems is a thing of the past.
