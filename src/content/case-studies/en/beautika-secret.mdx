## The Brand

Beautika Secret is one of Kuwait's emerging beauty retailers, offering curated skincare, makeup, and wellness products with a focus on premium quality and personal service. When they approached ENET, they had a vision but no digital infrastructure. We built it all.

## What We Delivered

### E-commerce Store

We designed and developed their flagship online store with a mobile-first approach, targeting customers across Kuwait and the GCC. The store features:

- **Smart inventory management** — centralised stock control with auto-sync across all sales channels
- **Customer loyalty programme** — reward points earned on every purchase, driving repeat sales
- **Seamless mobile checkout** — streamlined flow with saved addresses and one-click reorder
- **Product bundling** — promotional sets and gift collections with dynamic pricing
- **Bilingual Arabic & English** — full RTL layout support across every page

### Odoo ERP Implementation

Behind every beautiful storefront is an operation that needs to run smoothly. We implemented a full Odoo ERP suite covering:

- **Inventory & warehousing** — real-time stock visibility across all locations
- **Purchase management** — automated reorder points and supplier workflows
- **Sales & CRM** — pipeline tracking, customer records, and follow-up automation
- **Accounting & finance** — VAT-compliant invoicing and financial reporting
- **E-commerce integration** — live sync between the online store and Odoo inventory

### Corporate Website

To anchor the brand's professional identity, we built a corporate presence that communicates Beautika Secret's values to wholesale buyers, potential partners, and media contacts — separate from the retail store but sharing the same visual language.

## The Outcome

Beautika Secret launched with a complete digital ecosystem: a store that competes with regional beauty platforms, an ERP system that eliminates manual operations, and a corporate site that opens doors with partners and stockists. Returning customer rates climbed steadily in the first months following launch.
