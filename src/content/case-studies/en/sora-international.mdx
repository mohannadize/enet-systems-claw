## The Brand

Sora International is a Kuwait-based import and trading company handling diverse product categories sourced from international suppliers. Coordinating procurement, customs, warehousing, and sales through disparate tools had become a bottleneck to growth.

## What We Implemented

We implemented an Odoo ERP solution designed around the realities of import and trading:

- **Landed cost management** — full costing including freight, customs, and handling fees allocated per product
- **Multi-currency purchasing** — purchase orders and supplier invoices in foreign currencies with automatic conversion
- **Inventory valuation** — AVCO costing method with real-time margin visibility by product and category
- **Sales & pricing** — customer-specific pricelist management and quotation workflows
- **Import documentation tracking** — customs clearance stages mapped to purchase order status
- **Accounting & reconciliation** — bank reconciliation, foreign exchange gains/loss tracking, and financial reporting

## The Outcome

Sora International now has a single source of truth for every shipment from purchase order through to sale. Landed cost visibility transformed how they price products, improving margins across key categories.
