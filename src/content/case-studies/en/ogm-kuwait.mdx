## The Brand

OGM Kuwait operates in the professional services sector, where credibility, clarity, and first impressions matter enormously. Their existing web presence didn't reflect the quality of their work. We changed that.

## What We Built

We designed and developed a corporate website that communicates expertise and builds trust with prospective clients:

- **Strategic content architecture** — information hierarchy designed around the buyer's journey
- **Services showcase** — in-depth service pages that explain capabilities without jargon
- **Team and credentials** — leadership profiles and company credentials presented with gravitas
- **Case highlights** — selected project summaries to demonstrate real-world results
- **Enquiry system** — structured contact forms that qualify leads before they hit the inbox
- **Performance optimised** — fast loads on all connections, full Lighthouse audit pass

## The Outcome

OGM Kuwait's new site immediately improved how the brand is perceived in initial client conversations. The structured enquiry system increased qualified inbound contact by a measurable margin in the first quarter post-launch.
