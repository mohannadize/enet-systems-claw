## The Brand

Raz Car operates in Kuwait's competitive automotive market, offering car rental and fleet services. In a category where trust and presentation are everything, their online presence needed to work as hard as their team.

## What We Built

We created a high-performance corporate website designed to convert browsers into enquiries:

- **Fleet showcase** — rich vehicle listings with specifications, availability indicators, and comparison tools
- **Booking enquiry system** — structured request forms with date/duration selection feeding directly to the operations team
- **Mobile-optimised experience** — most car rental searches happen on mobile; we prioritised that journey
- **WhatsApp integration** — one-tap contact for customers who prefer a direct conversation
- **SEO-structured pages** — category and vehicle pages built for local search visibility
- **Trust signals** — customer testimonials, fleet size statistics, and certifications prominently placed

## The Outcome

Raz Car's web channel became a meaningful source of qualified leads within weeks of launch. The booking enquiry system significantly reduced back-and-forth on basic availability questions.
