## The Brand

Feeders Kuwait is a food distribution company supplying restaurants, hotels, and retailers across Kuwait. Fast-moving stock, tight margins, and complex logistics demanded an ERP solution that could keep pace.

## What We Implemented

We configured and deployed a full Odoo stack purpose-built for food distribution:

- **Inventory with expiry tracking** — FEFO rotation rules and lot traceability from supplier to end customer
- **Multi-location warehousing** — stock visibility across cold storage and ambient facilities
- **Route & delivery management** — optimised delivery scheduling and driver dispatch
- **Customer order management** — from quote to invoice with automated pricing rules per customer tier
- **Vendor management** — supplier scorecards, purchase lead times, and automated reorder triggers
- **Financial reporting** — P&L by product category, customer profitability analysis, and cash flow dashboards

## The Outcome

Feeders Kuwait gained complete traceability across their supply chain — something they previously couldn't achieve with disparate tools. Order fulfilment accuracy improved, and management now has real-time financial visibility they can act on.
