## Overview

We've delivered e-commerce platforms for fashion brands, beauty retailers, automotive dealers, and multi-category marketplaces around the world. Every store we build is designed around two priorities: making it easy for customers to buy, and making it easy for your team to operate.

## What's Included

### Storefront and UX

- Mobile-first, fast-loading product pages
- Advanced filtering by category, brand, price, and product attributes
- Bilingual and multilingual storefronts with full RTL layout when needed
- Editorial collections and lookbooks for lifestyle brands

### Payments and Checkout

- Integration with major payment gateways and international processors
- Streamlined, one-page checkout with saved addresses
- Buy Now Pay Later options

### Operations

- Real-time inventory management with low-stock alerts and automated reorder triggers
- Multi-warehouse and multi-location stock control
- Order management and fulfilment workflows
- Returns and refunds processing

### Marketing Tools

- Customer loyalty and points programmes
- Promotional pricing, flash sales, and coupon management
- Abandoned cart recovery emails
- SEO-ready product catalogue structure

## Technologies

We work with Shopify, WooCommerce, and fully custom-built storefronts depending on your business requirements and existing stack. For large catalogues and high-traffic scenarios we recommend a headless approach.
