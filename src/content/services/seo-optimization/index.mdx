## Search Visibility That Compounds

Paid ads stop the moment you stop paying. SEO builds an asset — one that generates qualified traffic every day without an ongoing ad budget. We design and execute SEO strategies that produce durable, measurable results across Arabic and English search results.

## Technical SEO

A website that search engines cannot crawl or index will never rank, no matter how good the content is. We start with a thorough technical audit to eliminate the structural barriers holding your site back.

What we fix:

- Crawlability issues — broken links, orphan pages, incorrect robots.txt, and XML sitemap errors
- Indexation problems — duplicate content, canonicalisation, and hreflang for bilingual sites
- Site architecture — logical URL structures and internal linking that distribute authority efficiently
- Core Web Vitals — Largest Contentful Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift, and Interaction to Next Paint scores that meet Google's page experience thresholds
- Structured data — schema markup for products, services, FAQs, and local businesses

## On-Page Optimisation

Ranking requires content that satisfies searcher intent better than the competition. We optimise every page for the specific queries your customers are typing — in both Arabic and English.

Key activities:

- Keyword research mapped to the buyer journey
- Title tags, meta descriptions, and heading hierarchy
- Content gap analysis against ranking competitors
- Internal linking to strengthen topical authority
- Image optimisation and alt-text for accessibility and search

## Local & Arabic SEO

Kuwait, KSA, and the wider GCC run on Google — and Google increasingly prioritises local and language-matched results. We optimise your presence for the markets that matter to you.

- Google Business Profile setup, optimisation, and management
- Arabic keyword research — MSA and dialect variations where relevant
- Citation building across regional directories
- RTL-ready on-page content and metadata

## Link Building & Authority

Backlinks remain one of Google's strongest ranking signals. We build links that editors and journalists actually want to give — through digital PR, resource content, and strategic outreach.

Our approach:

- Competitor backlink analysis to find proven link opportunities
- Content creation designed to earn links naturally
- Outreach to relevant regional and international publications
- Disavow management for toxic links that harm authority

## Reporting & Measurement

SEO without tracking is guesswork. We connect Google Search Console, GA4, and rank-tracking tools to give you full visibility into what is working and why.

Every month you receive:

- Ranking movements by keyword cluster
- Organic traffic and conversion trends
- Technical health score
- Next 30-day priority actions
