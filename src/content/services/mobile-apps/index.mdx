## Mobile-First Thinking

The majority of your customers are on their phones. Whether that means a consumer app on the App Store or a purpose-built internal tool for your field team, we build mobile applications that are fast, intuitive, and reliable.

## Native vs Cross-Platform

We help you choose the right approach for your project:

- **Native iOS (Swift)** — maximum performance and deep platform integration for apps that need it.
- **Native Android (Kotlin)** — full access to the Android ecosystem with platform-native behaviour.
- **Cross-platform (Flutter, React Native)** — a single codebase for both platforms, reducing cost and keeping feature parity. Our preferred choice for most projects where native-only capabilities aren't required.

## What We Build

### Consumer Apps

Customer-facing mobile experiences for e-commerce, loyalty programmes, booking systems, and service delivery — designed to be fast, accessible, and easy to use.

### Business and Operations Apps

Internal tools for your field team, warehouse staff, sales reps, or delivery drivers. Mobile access to your ERP, inventory system, or CRM — with offline capability where connectivity is unreliable.

### App Store Optimisation

Getting an app built is only the start. We handle submission to the App Store and Google Play, write optimised metadata, and advise on ratings and review strategy to improve visibility and conversion from the store listing.

## Design and UX

Our design process starts with real users. We conduct user research, create wireframes, and iterate on interactive prototypes before a single line of code is written. The result is an app your users understand immediately.

## Maintenance and Ongoing Updates

Mobile platforms evolve fast. We offer ongoing maintenance plans to keep your app compatible with the latest iOS and Android releases, address performance regressions, and ship new features as your business grows.
