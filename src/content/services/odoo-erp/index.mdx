## Official Odoo Partner

ENET is a certified Odoo partner with a track record of successful ERP implementations across trading, retail, agriculture, healthcare, and professional services in Kuwait and the broader MENA region.

Odoo is a comprehensive, modular ERP platform covering every operational domain of a business — from accounting and inventory to sales, CRM, HR, and manufacturing. We configure, customise, and implement it to match exactly how your business works.

## What We Cover

### Implementation

We handle the full implementation lifecycle: requirements analysis, system design, configuration, data migration, user acceptance testing, and go-live support. No off-the-shelf templates — every implementation is scoped to your specific processes.

### Module Customisation

When standard Odoo modules don't match your workflow, we build custom ones. We've developed industry-specific modules for supply chain, healthcare billing, agricultural production tracking, and more.

### Data Migration

Moving from spreadsheets, legacy systems, or another ERP? We extract, clean, transform, and load your historical data into Odoo — inventory records, customer databases, transaction history — so you go live with a full picture.

### Integrations

We integrate Odoo with third-party systems your business depends on: payment gateways, shipping carriers, e-commerce storefronts, barcode scanners, government compliance APIs, and custom internal tools.

### Training and Support

We don't disappear after go-live. We run structured training sessions for your team, provide documentation, and offer ongoing support to handle questions, refinements, and new module rollouts as your business grows.

### Cloud Hosting

We can host and manage your Odoo instance on a reliable cloud infrastructure with automated backups, uptime monitoring, and regular version updates.
