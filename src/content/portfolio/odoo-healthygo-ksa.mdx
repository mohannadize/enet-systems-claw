## Overview

HealthyGO KSA operates healthcare facilities and needed an ERP system capable of handling the regulatory and operational complexity of the healthcare sector. We implemented a customised Odoo solution that connects clinical, pharmacy, and financial workflows in a single system.

## What We Implemented

The implementation required careful attention to healthcare-specific requirements including insurance billing standards, patient data handling, and integration with the pharmacy management layer.

Key modules and customisations:

- **Patient management** — structured patient records with appointment scheduling, visit history, and document attachments.
- **Pharmacy integration** — connected pharmacy module managing drug inventory, prescription fulfilment, and dispensing records.
- **Insurance billing** — configurable insurance schemes with claim generation, submission tracking, and reconciliation.
- **Clinical workflow** — appointment-to-consultation-to-prescription workflow supporting the care team's day-to-day operations.
- **Financial management** — revenue tracking across service lines, cost-centre accounting, and financial reporting.
- **Compliance reporting** — structured data exports to meet health-authority reporting requirements.

## Result

HealthyGO KSA gained an integrated operational platform that connects every part of the patient journey to the financial and administrative back office. Insurance claim processing time and billing errors both improved significantly after go-live.
