## Overview

Najm Al Tamayoz is an established construction and contracting company. They needed a professional website that communicates their track record and capabilities to new clients and tender committees. We built a structured company profile that lets their portfolio of work speak for itself.

## What We Built

The site balances visual impact — showcasing completed projects through photography and data — with the practical information a prospective client or procurement team needs to qualify the company.

Key capabilities include:

- **Project portfolio gallery** — filterable showcase of completed construction projects with photography, scope descriptions, and delivery timelines.
- **Certifications and accreditations** — clearly presented quality and safety credentials that support tender submissions.
- **Client testimonials** — curated feedback from past clients, adding third-party credibility.
- **Company history timeline** — visual narrative of the firm's growth and milestones.
- **Arabic and English** — full bilingual experience for international visitors.

## Result

Najm Al Tamayoz can now point prospects to a polished digital portfolio that demonstrates their experience and professionalism, strengthening their position in competitive tender processes.
