## Overview

Feeders Kuwait operates a large-scale supply chain serving the food and hospitality sector. Managing demand variability and logistics complexity across their network required a purpose-configured ERP solution. We implemented Odoo with supply chain–focused customisations that turned their operations data into actionable decisions.

## What We Implemented

The implementation tackled the two biggest challenges in their business: predicting demand accurately and tracking product movement reliably across the supply chain.

Key modules and customisations:

- **Demand forecasting** — configurable forecasting models that account for seasonality and historical consumption patterns to drive procurement planning.
- **Automated reordering** — min/max reorder rules with lead-time awareness that generate purchase orders before stockouts occur.
- **Logistics tracking** — end-to-end shipment tracking from supplier dispatch through customer delivery.
- **Supplier portal** — self-service portal for key suppliers to confirm orders, update delivery schedules, and upload documents.
- **Quality control checkpoints** — defined inspection steps for inbound goods with pass/fail recording.
- **KPI dashboards** — management view of fill rates, lead times, and supplier performance.

## Result

Feeders Kuwait gained the visibility and automation needed to run a responsive supply chain. Stockout incidents dropped significantly, and procurement planning that previously took days now runs on a reliable automated cycle.
