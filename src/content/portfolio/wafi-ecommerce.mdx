## Overview

Wafi is one of Kuwait's most recognised retail brands. We built their flagship multi-category e-commerce platform to handle thousands of concurrent shoppers across home, fashion, electronics, and lifestyle departments.

## What We Built

Scale and reliability were the top priorities. The platform is built to handle traffic spikes during promotional events without sacrificing performance or customer experience.

Key capabilities include:

- **Multi-category catalogue** — unified browsing across diverse product departments with category-specific filtering.
- **Real-time inventory** — live stock sync ensures accurate availability across warehouse and store locations.
- **Order tracking** — end-to-end visibility from placement through delivery with automated notifications.
- **Promotions engine** — configurable discounts, flash sales, and coupon management by the marketing team.
- **Customer accounts** — order history, saved wishlists, and address book for returning customers.

## Result

Wafi's digital storefront became a primary revenue channel, serving thousands of customers reliably. The platform has scaled smoothly through major sales events including Ramadan and National Day promotions.
