## Overview

Sora International is a trading company with complex procurement and logistics requirements that go beyond what standard Odoo modules offer out of the box. We developed custom Odoo modules to match their specific workflows and integrated the system with their shipping partners.

## What We Implemented

The project combined a standard Odoo implementation with bespoke development to handle Sora's unique operational requirements.

Key modules and customisations:

- **Automated procurement** — rules-based purchase order generation triggered by stock levels, sales forecasts, and lead times.
- **Vendor management** — structured vendor profiles with performance scoring, contract terms, and approved product lists.
- **Shipping integrations** — direct integration with shipping carriers for booking, label generation, and tracking status updates.
- **Custom import workflows** — tailored module handling Sora's import documentation and customs compliance process.
- **Multi-currency procurement** — purchase orders in supplier currencies with automated exchange rate handling.
- **Management dashboards** — live overview of open orders, pending shipments, and payables.

## Result

Sora International's procurement cycle became faster and more reliable. Automated reordering eliminated most stockout situations, and the shipping integration removed the manual data entry that had been causing errors and delays.
