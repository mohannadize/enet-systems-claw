## Overview

Beautika Secret operates multiple retail locations and needed a point-of-sale and inventory system that could keep all of them in sync. We implemented Odoo's retail suite, tailored to the specific workflows of a beauty and cosmetics business.

## What We Implemented

The implementation focused on giving floor staff a fast, reliable POS experience while giving the operations team complete visibility across all locations.

Key modules and customisations:

- **Odoo POS** — fast, touch-friendly point-of-sale interface configured for beauty retail workflows.
- **Multi-location inventory** — centralised stock control with per-location visibility and inter-location transfer management.
- **Real-time stock synchronisation** — sales at any location instantly update the central inventory.
- **Product variants** — handling of shades, sizes, and product variants without catalogue complexity.
- **Promotions and discounts** — configurable promotional pricing and loyalty discounts applied automatically at the till.
- **End-of-day reporting** — automated daily sales summaries and cash reconciliation reports per location.

## Result

Beautika Secret's operations team gained a clear, accurate picture of stock across all their stores for the first time. Shrinkage was reduced, and stockouts on popular products became rare thanks to better reorder visibility.
