## Overview

The Wafi Group needed a corporate web presence separate from their retail storefront — one that speaks to investors, partners, and the media rather than shoppers. We built a professional corporate profile that reflects the group's stature and long history in Kuwait.

## What We Built

The site is structured around the needs of multiple audiences: investors seeking financial information, media looking for press materials, and the public interested in the group's CSR commitments.

Key capabilities include:

- **Investor relations section** — governance documents, annual reports, and board composition presented clearly and accessibly.
- **News centre** — managed press releases, media coverage, and company announcements with category filtering.
- **Corporate social responsibility showcase** — dedicated section highlighting the group's community investment programmes and sustainability initiatives.
- **Leadership profiles** — executive team directory with structured bios.
- **Bilingual (Arabic / English)** — full content parity in both languages, with proper RTL support.

## Result

Wafi Group now has a credible, well-structured digital front for corporate stakeholders — distinct from the consumer-facing brand and purpose-built for the audiences that matter most to the holding company.
