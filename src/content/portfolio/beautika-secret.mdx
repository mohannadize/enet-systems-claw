## Overview

Beautika Secret is a Kuwait-based beauty retailer offering skincare, makeup, and wellness products. We designed and developed their e-commerce presence from the ground up, prioritising a smooth shopping experience and strong back-office tools for the operations team.

## What We Built

The store combines a visually appealing storefront with a robust inventory and order management backbone.

Key capabilities include:

- **Smart inventory management** — centralised stock control with auto-sync across all sales channels.
- **Customer loyalty programme** — reward points earned on every purchase, driving repeat sales.
- **Seamless checkout** — streamlined, mobile-first checkout with saved addresses and one-click reorder.
- **Product bundling** — promotional sets and gift collections with dynamic pricing.
- **Arabic & English support** — bilingual product content and RTL layout.

## Result

Beautika Secret launched with a store that competes with established regional beauty platforms while maintaining the personal, boutique feel of the brand. Returning customer rates climbed steadily in the months following launch.
