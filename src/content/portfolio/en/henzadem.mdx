## Overview

Henzadem is a fashion and lifestyle brand that wanted a shopping experience as distinctive as its collections. We built a modern e-commerce platform that blends editorial aesthetics with smart commerce features.

## What We Built

The platform puts product discovery at the centre of the experience, using curated trend categories and personalised recommendations to keep shoppers engaged.

Key capabilities include:

- **Virtual try-on** — browser-based overlay tool letting customers visualise products before purchase.
- **Intelligent size guides** — measurement-based size recommendations that reduce returns.
- **Trend-based recommendations** — personalised product suggestions powered by browsing behaviour.
- **Editorial collections** — curated lookbooks that merchandise products in lifestyle context.
- **Mobile-first design** — optimised for the majority of shoppers who browse on their phones.

## Result

Henzadem launched with a storefront that sets them apart in the regional fashion market. The virtual try-on feature drove measurable improvements in conversion rate and a meaningful reduction in return requests.
