## Overview

Wafi General Trading required a comprehensive ERP system to unify operations across their trading business. We implemented a full Odoo suite covering every major operational domain, connecting processes that were previously managed across disconnected tools.

## What We Implemented

The implementation was designed for operational efficiency — reducing manual data entry, improving accuracy, and giving management real-time visibility into the business.

Key modules and customisations:

- **Inventory management** — multi-warehouse stock control with batch tracking, expiry management, and automated reordering.
- **Accounting and finance** — full chart of accounts, multi-currency transactions, and consolidated financial reporting.
- **Sales and CRM** — quotation-to-invoice workflow with customer pricing tiers and sales team tracking.
- **Purchase management** — vendor management, purchase orders, and three-way matching against deliveries and invoices.
- **Barcode scanning** — integrated barcode workflows for receiving, picking, and stocktaking operations.
- **Automated reporting** — scheduled reports delivered to management for inventory turnover, sales performance, and financial summaries.

## Result

Wafi General Trading moved from fragmented spreadsheets and manual processes to a single, integrated system. The time spent on month-end close and stock reconciliation dropped substantially after go-live.
