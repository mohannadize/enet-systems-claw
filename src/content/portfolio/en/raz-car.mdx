## Overview

Raz Car is an automotive dealership that needed a website capable of doing more than just showcasing vehicles — it needed to actively generate qualified leads and streamline the pre-sales process. We built a conversion-focused dealership platform with deep integration into their operations.

## What We Built

The site brings together a comprehensive vehicle showcase with transactional tools that move prospects further along the buying journey before they even visit the showroom.

Key capabilities include:

- **Live vehicle inventory** — browseable listings with filters for make, model, year, price, and fuel type, synced with the dealership's stock system.
- **Financing calculators** — interactive EMI and down-payment calculators that give buyers immediate, personalised cost estimates.
- **Appointment booking** — integrated scheduling system for test drives and service appointments, directly notifying the sales team.
- **Vehicle comparison** — side-by-side comparison of up to four models across key specifications.
- **Mobile-first design** — optimised for customers browsing on smartphones during their research phase.

## Result

Raz Car's website transformed their online presence into a genuine lead generation channel. Test drive bookings through the site increased significantly in the first quarter after launch.
