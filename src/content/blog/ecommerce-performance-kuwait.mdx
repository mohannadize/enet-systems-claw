Launch day is not the finish line. For retailers competing with quick delivery expectations and mobile-heavy traffic, performance is part of the product.

## What to measure first

- **Time to interactive** on mid-tier phones on 4G, not only lab scores on Wi-Fi.
- **Checkout completion rate** from cart to paid order, split by payment method.
- **Server response under load** during campaigns and peak shopping periods.

## Patterns we see work

- Aggressive **image optimisation** and modern formats for product galleries.
- **Minimal third-party scripts** on checkout paths.
- **Caching and CDN** tuned for real customer latency, not only origin server speed.

## Content and trust

Fast pages help, but clarity wins: shipping zones, return rules, and copy that reads naturally in every language you sell in all reduce hesitation at checkout.

If you are planning a replatform or a performance pass on an existing store, start with real user monitoring on your top landing and product templates — that is where fixes pay off fastest.
