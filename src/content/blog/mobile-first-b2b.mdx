B2B software used to mean desktop-only ERP screens and PDF printouts. That model breaks down the moment your warehouse lead checks stock from the floor or a sales rep confirms availability from a client car park.

## Design for one-handed tasks

Primary actions should sit in thumb reach. Dense data tables need responsive layouts or dedicated mobile views, not horizontal scroll nightmares.

## Offline and flaky networks

Short disconnects are normal on the road. Queue writes where possible and show clear sync status so users trust the app when they are back online.

## One stack, multiple surfaces

Whether you extend Odoo with mobile-friendly flows or ship a companion app, the goal is the same: **the same business rules everywhere**, with interfaces tuned to the device.

We build web and mobile experiences on top of the same backends our clients already run. If your team is fighting desktop-only tools in the field, it is worth a conversation about what a mobile-first layer would look like for your workflows.
